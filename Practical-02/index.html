About Our Department

The Faculty of Computer Applications at Marwadi University focuses on developing students' knowledge in computer science, programming, software development and modern technologies.

Our department encourages practical learning, creativity, teamwork and problem-solving skills to prepare students for the technology industry.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in computer education by developing innovative, skilled and responsible technology professionals.

Our Mission

  1. Provide quality education in computer applications.
  2. Develop practical programming and technical skills.
  3. Encourage innovation and research.
  4. Prepare students for real-world industry challenges.
  5. Promote teamwork and ethical values.

Academic Programs

  • Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
  • Computer Programming
  • Web Development
  • Database Management
  • Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals
